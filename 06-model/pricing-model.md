## Stage + model

**Stage:** Stage 2 · Revenue Expansion, _(not filled in)_

**Model:** Freemium, I think that freemium model is the right model here even though we loose a little bit short-term revenue. With this model we can build a little bit a habit and help users to recognize the value. This helps us by scaling As long as the value is recognized we can start the monetization.

_____

## Recommendation

Move from a free trial to a reverse-trial freemium model: every new user gets 2 months of premium, then drops to a usable free tier that gates the advanced training plans they've started relying on.

**The pricing bet:** Packaging matched to value, validating that the free/paid line sits where users feel they need more.
