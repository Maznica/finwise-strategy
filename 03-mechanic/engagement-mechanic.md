# The Mechanic · FinWise

> Module 3 · Retention & Engagement. The engagement mechanic that builds the habit loop, with rationale and a wireframe.

## The mechanic

**Mechanic:** Custom

- **Trigger:** Jana (accountant) finishes her review of last month and marks it done in her own client-book view. That click pushes to the owner, carrying her actual words: “Jana: Your October ad spend doubled — Christmas campaign?” Owner-initiated path, equally valid: FinWise flags an anomaly, the owner asks, and the owner’s question becomes the trigger for Jana.
- **Action:** The owner-side half the mechanic reinforces is answering or resolving an item Jana raised — anchored to a specific transaction or line, never a free-floating “looks fine.”
- **Reward:** The item moves from Waiting on you to Waiting on Jana; your column decrements. Immediate, physical, and it’s a relief reward rather than a points reward.
- **Investment:** Closing the item writes it to the record: “Sept — outflow exceeded inflow by £1,760 → Q4 stock buy, Jana approved.” This is the Hook Model crossover. The context is the owner’s own explanation of their own business, which makes FinWise more valuable to this owner specifically — and it makes the next trigger land harder, because the thread now has history.

## Rationale

I have chosen a some new mechanic - "Whose Turn" with combination of progress bar as none of the free standard mechanics fit cleanly. 
Whose Turn is a persistent, repairable, two-sided state: Waiting on you · Waiting on Jana (accountant), each item visibly ageing. It taps loss aversion, but socially anchored — the loss isn’t a number resetting, it’s being the person holding things up.

The churn diagnosis it answers. Module 1’s data killed the obvious mechanism: deep feature adoption rose 5.4× (Modeling 10.78% → 58.26%) while trial→paid moved 0.01 points, and churn outran new customers in all 13 months (317 vs 128). Engagement went up and bought nothing. The missing ingredient was never engagement — it was dependency. Any mechanic that a solo owner can complete alone reproduces that failure exactly, which is why the progress bar lost: it can hit 100% without the accountant.

Whose Turn cannot be cleared alone. There are no items without a second person. The metric it moves is dependency, not activity.
