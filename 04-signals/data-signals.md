## Leading indicators

- **Share of people started the imported data (connection to the bank):** It is the hard gate on the North Star — no user can reach a modeling output without passing it, so every point gained here is a point of North Star volume, and it responds within days of any onboarding change.
- **Import - first modeling output rate with good data (%modelling rate):** It is the second multiplicative term in the North Star — of those who do import, how many actually reach a good output. Unfortunately in my prototype leading 1 and 2 are collapsed as the model is generated automatically.

_____

## Lagging indicators

- **Trial to paid conversion rate:** It is the business outcome the whole scenario is stalled on, it can’t be moved directly, and it’s the only number that proves the activation work converted into revenue at scale rather than just producing busier trials.
