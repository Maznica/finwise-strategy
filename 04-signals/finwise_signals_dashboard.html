Trial-to-paid has a standard deviation of 0.063 percentage points on a mean of 1.99% — a coefficient of variation of 3.2%. Correlation measures co-variation, so when one side does not vary, r is near zero by construction regardless of the true causal link. With n = 13 the confidence interval spans strong-negative to strong-positive, and the monthly aggregate is an ecological correlation: the trials importing in month M are not the trials converting in month M. The cohort-level test — do trials who import convert better than trials who do not — is the one that answers the question.
